## Labeling rule
Compliant = hardhat and a reflective high-visibility vest. Workers who are
tiny, cropped or mostly hidden, and bystanders, were not counted. Labels
were made by visual inspection before running the scripts.
Key v1 counted vest-colored garments without reflectors as compliant.
